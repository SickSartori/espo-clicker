# 💥 Espòòò Clicker - Official Release Notes 💥

## Version 3.2: Trick or Technical Debt? 🎃

Halloween has reached the office. From October 24 to November 2 the lights go down, something that should not be there is roaming between the desks, and Espò has dug out the costumes. Under the costume, the usual maintenance work: cloud saves now cope with numbers of any size, and two open tabs no longer step on each other's toes.

---

### 🎃 Halloween at the Office — October 24 to November 2

* **🎭 Three Costumes:** **The Espostor**, **Frankenespò** and **Count Espòcula**. What they look like you find out by unlocking them; we can tell you that one of the three is not who he seems. The Espostor is a gift: just redeem the **Trick or Treat** achievement, active for the whole event. The other two are in the Shop for **10 Tokens**.
* **🕯️ The Office Changes Its Look:** Put a costume on and everything around it changes: night purple and pumpkin orange, cobwebs in the corners, a row of candles flickering under the bar and a few little ghosts drifting slowly up to the ceiling. If you asked your system for less motion, the ghosts stay home and everything else holds still.
* **🕷️ The Cursed Bug:** With a costume on, the Golden Bug is not golden anymore. You catch them the same way and they pay the same; only the face looking back at you changes.
* **🎵 A Soundtrack in Costume:** The Espòòò Clicker theme has its own Halloween version. It starts on its own with the costumes, you can pick it from the music settings as soon as you own one, and it is in the jukebox too.
* **🗓️ Nothing Disappears When It Ends:** After November 2 the achievement retires, but the costumes stay in the Shop at full price — **20 Tokens** for the Espostor, **25** for the other two. Whoever got them keeps them forever. No skin in this game is lost for showing up late.

---

### 🏷️ One Rarity for Every Holiday

* **🎁 Festive, for Every Season:** Skins tied to a time of year all sit under the **Festive** rarity: the three Halloween costumes and, as before, Espo Claus. In the wardrobe they filter together.
* **🎨 The Holiday Picks the Colour:** Same rarity, different colours: pumpkin orange for Halloween, red and snowflakes for Christmas. It applies to the wardrobe card, to the avatar border on the leaderboard and among friends, and to the background behind Espò.

---

### 🕹️ Arcade: Bug Invaders Dresses Up Too

* **👻 Ghosts, Pumpkins and Tombstones:** For the whole event the Bug Invaders swarm comes down in costume: ghosts in the top rows, carved pumpkins at the bottom, tombstones instead of bunkers and a full moon in the background.
* **🎯 Only the Look Changes:** Speed, shots and scoring are the usual ones: a record set on Halloween counts as much as any other.

---

### 🗂️ One Tab at a Time

* **🔒 Two Tabs, Only One Saves:** Opening the game in two tabs of the same browser, both saved to the same save, and the one left behind could overwrite the progress made in the other. Now only the last one you opened saves; the other pauses and tells you so.
* **▶️ Play Here:** To pick up in the paused tab, one tap on **Play here** is enough — or simply go back to it: it reloads and starts exactly where you got to in the other one.
* **📱 And Across Different Devices:** A phone and a computer cannot talk to each other like that. There the 3.1 safety net stays in place: if the cloud reads as ahead three times in a row, the game stops and tells you, instead of realigning over and over.

---

### ☁️ Cloud Saves

* **🔢 The Big Number Wall:** Nobody had hit this one yet, and now nobody will. Past 309 zeros of score, the server could no longer read the number and **cloud saves would have stopped for good** — no message, no way out. Alongside it, the leaderboard would have shuffled the top places and the anti-rollback check would have called two wildly different scores "equal". All three now compare scores digit by digit, with no size limit. A bonus for everyone, not just for those who get up there: the comparison is now exact on today's numbers too, where two scores less than 65,000 apart used to read as identical.
* **🔎 The Server Says Against What:** When the cloud rejects a save because it reads as ahead, the line in the browser console now carries the server's numbers too: score, Promotions, Formats and season it compared against, and how long ago. If you write to us about a sync problem, pasting it tells us everything in one go.
