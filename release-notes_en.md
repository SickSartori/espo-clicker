# 💥 Espòòò Clicker - Official Release Notes 💥

## Version 3.1: A New Cabinet 🕹️

The "??? COMING SOON" slot in the Arcade is a mystery no longer. Plus the usual round of maintenance: the game on phones properly tidied up, the Friends tab finally behaving as it should, event audio levelled out, and the reports you sent us closed one by one. Keep them coming — that is how they end up here.

---

### 🕹️ New Game: STACK OVERFLOW

* **📦 Stack the Code:** The Arcade's seventh cabinet. Slot the code pieces together, complete rows and ship them to production. So far so easy.
* **📉 Technical Debt Rises:** Every so many pieces a debt row surfaces from the bottom and pushes the whole stack up. The higher your level, the more often it comes. You cannot ignore it — you can only stay ahead of it.
* **🐛 Bugs Jam the Row:** A completed row containing a bug **will not clear**. You have to squash it — with a click, the way this game has always done things. Which is why one hand never leaves the mouse.
* **🧩 Eight Pieces, Not the Ones You Expect:** PIPELINE, MERGE, BRANCH, REBASE, CONFLICT and friends. A couple of them are only three squares, which changes the maths quite a bit.
* **💀 How It Ends:** The stack hits the ceiling and overflows. The name was a hint.

---

### 📱 On Phones: Bar and Windows Tidied Up

* **☰ A Menu for the Secondary Entries:** The bar had grown to eight mute icons, indistinguishable at a glance. Now only the entries that actually tell you something stay in the bar — Promotion with its percentage, Profile with its notifications — and everything else lives in a ☰ menu where each one carries **its own icon and its own name**.
* **🆘 Help and Report Finally Reachable:** On phones the Help button was hidden, and Help is where you go to report something. In practice, playing on a phone meant no way to write to us. Now there is one.
* **🖼️ Every Window Full Screen:** No more exceptions. And when the content is short it sits centred, instead of clinging to the top with half the screen empty.
* **❌ A Close Button on Settings Too:** It was the only window without an exit at the top: you could only close it with the button at the very bottom, after scrolling all the way. Now there is an X — and it saves, exactly like the button does.
* **🎨 The Logo in the Middle:** With the entries moved into the menu, the centre of the bar was left empty. Espò lives there now.

---

### 👥 Friends: the Tab Behaves as It Should

* **🕐 Real Playtime:** A friend's profile always showed zero playtime. For everyone, always. It now shows the hours actually played.
* **🔔 Notifications on Time:** The badge for requests and messages turned up well after you signed in, and you often only discovered it by opening your Profile. It now appears as soon as you are in the game.
* **🖱️ The Whole Row Opens:** Opening a friend's profile meant hitting the little arrow on the right. Now tapping anywhere on the row works.
* **🙋 Sensible Suggestions:** People you had already invited kept showing up under Suggested with a "pending" tag — a suggestion you cannot act on. Suggestions now only list people you can actually add. Search them by name, though, and the state of the request is still there: that is where it matters.

---

### ⚖️ Formatting and Balance

* **💎 Q-Bits as Promised:** The Q-bits announced by Formatting dropped as you spent Tokens in the Promotion shop: you could be promised 10 and collect 3. The count is now based on the Tokens **earned** during the cycle, not on what is left in your pocket — spending them no longer costs you anything.
* **🐛 Bug Bounty Toned Down:** The upgrade was worth +20% per level with **no ceiling**, and Critical Tickets quickly became more profitable than anything else: on their own they were enough to buy the endgame teams far sooner than intended. It is now +10% per level and stops at 10 levels. Anyone already past that keeps their levels, but the multiplier is smaller: chasing Critical Tickets is still worth it, just no longer decisive.
* **💰 Bug Bounty Costs More:** The ceiling fixed the endgame, not the opening: the first level was paid for with the loose change of your first few Promotions, and by then most of the bonus was already yours. The base cost goes from 75 to 250 Tokens. What it is worth has not changed — still +10% per level, still up to 10 — what changes is **when** you can afford it: roughly two Promotions later, at every level.
* **☄️ The MADE IN HEAVEN Button Is All There:** The icon ended up on a line of its own, squashed against the top edge of the button. It now sits next to the label, the way it does on the Sign Contract button.
* **👑 Founders Keep ALL Their Skins:** At launch a Founder saved only **five**, picked from a list: someone with thirty ended up with seven. The cap is gone. Skins are purely cosmetic — no bonuses, no multipliers — so there was no balance to protect, and taking them away was just a loss. Anyone yet to return will find their wardrobe **intact**; the Season 1 reset stays where it belongs: scores, leaderboard and achievements.

---

### 🔊 Event Volume Finally Levelled Out

* **📢 No More Whispered Videos:** Some event videos played much quieter than the rest — Rick Espley and the two Ricardo Milespo versions above all. Every track has been brought to the same level: switching from one event to another will no longer send you running for the volume knob.
* **🎚️ Levels in Order:** We also fixed the opposite case, a video that risked distorting in its loudest moments.

---

### 🎯 Achievements Readable on Mobile

* **📱 Full Titles:** On phones the longer achievement names were cut in half with an ellipsis. They now wrap onto a second line and can be read in full.
* **📊 The Percentage No Longer Clipped:** The progress label overflowed the card and only its tail stayed visible: "60% (3 / 5)" read as "3 / 5)". It now fits.

---

### 🎬 Cleaner Video Events

* **✨ A Truly Clear Screen:** During event videos the last decorative bits that lingered on top of the footage now disappear too. The screen is properly clear.

---

### 🚪 The Arcade: Getting In, Getting Out, and Super Espò

* **🪟 It Opens Even When Popups Are Blocked:** The Arcade opens in a new tab, and on phones browsers often block it. When that happened nothing opened — and nobody told you why. It now falls back to the current tab.
* **🔇 Cabinets Properly Switched Off:** Leaving the Arcade left some games running in the background. They all stop now.
* **⭐ The Star No Longer Leaves You See-Through:** In Super Espò, grabbing a Super Star right after a hit — while Espò was still flashing — left the character half-transparent for the whole twelve seconds of invincibility. It now snaps back to full opacity.

---

### ☁️ Progress and Reports

* **🔄 The Sync Notice Answers Back:** When your progress was not reaching the cloud, a notice appeared that seemed to do nothing when tapped and simply stayed there. It now tells you what it is doing and how it went — and if the problem is an expired session, it takes you straight to the login.
* **📮 Where to Report Things:** Once, and only once, after the release notes, we show you where the Report tab is and what is worth writing in it. Half the fixes above came from there.
