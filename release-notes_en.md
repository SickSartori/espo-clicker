# 💥 Espòòò Clicker - Official Release Notes 💥

## Version 3.0: Cyber-Tech Rebirth ⚡

A radical restyling of the whole interface, animations rebuilt from scratch, more modern modals and a flood of refinements that give the game a new soul without betraying its longtime identity.

---

### 🎨 Cyberpunk-Tech Interface (UI 3.0)

* **🖼️ Complete Restyle:** New visual identity with cyan/neon tones, strategic glows, tech monospace fonts and HUD details everywhere.
* **🎯 Modern Icons:** The navbar, tab, header and modal icons have been replaced with a cleaner, more legible set.
* **📐 Cleaner Layout:** Revised spacing, clearer visual hierarchy and improved contrast across every panel.

---

### 🚀 Reworked Clicker

* **🔘 Real 3D Effect:** The clicker button now has a true depth effect with a dynamic bevel that follows the mouse position.
* **🌐 Full-Screen Parallax:** Button tilt based on mouse movement across the whole page, not just the clicker area.
* **✨ Smoother Animations:** More responsive clicks, more natural transitions, instant visual feedback.

---

### 👗 Completely Rewritten Wardrobe

* **🆕 Single View:** Removed the grid/carousel toggle. Now a single view with all skins always visible.
* **🔍 Click for Preview:** Clicking a skin opens a panel with its enlarged image, description, cost and equip button.
* **⚡ Quick Equip:** A small button on each unlocked skin lets you equip it on the fly, without opening the preview.
* **🎨 Rarity-Themed Backgrounds:** Every skin card has an animated HUD background based on rarity (grid, scan, hex, rays, aurora, snow...).
* **🏷️ Always-Visible Names:** Even locked skins show the name and a mysterious silhouette, to spark curiosity.
* **🔒 Visual Lock:** New overlay with a lock icon for skins not yet unlocked.

---

### 🪟 Refreshed Modals

* **🔧 Stable Windows:** Fixed the "flicker" on open: modals now appear with a smooth transition and no jumps.
* **💎 Cyber-Tech Login:** Login screen with an animated logo halo, HUD inputs with focus effects and a glowing sweep button.
* **⚙️ More Compact Options:** Optimized settings layout with inline sliders, a card per section and more immediate actions.
* **👤 Leaner Account Management:** Account modal redesigned in a compact format with a more recognizable "Critical Area" section.
* **🏆 Updated Achievements & Stats:** More elegant trophy cards, stat boxes with cyan accent, clearer navigation.
* **🎨 Colored Accent by Type:** Each modal has a subtle colored bar at the top (gold prestige, purple format, green leaderboard...).
* **🌫️ Covering Login Background:** When the login is open, the game behind it is blurred for greater focus.

---

### 🔔 Rebuilt Notifications (Toasts)

* **📍 Fixed Positions:** Toasts no longer shift when new ones arrive. Each notification takes a dedicated slot.
* **🎀 Compact Design:** Smaller, gentler toasts, with a lifetime bar and a color-coded icon per type.
* **🖱️ Click to Close:** A single click dismisses a toast immediately.
* **⏸️ Pause on Hover:** Hovering over a toast stops the timer — handy for reading it properly.

---

### 🐛 Reinvented Golden Bug

* **✨ Glitch Effect:** The bug now has a real glitch look — chromatic distortions, scanlines and visual bursts that evoke the idea of a "bug".
* **⚡ Fast Animations:** Since it stays on screen only briefly, all its animations are quicker and more impactful.
* **🚨 Despawn Warning:** In the final seconds before disappearing, it flashes urgently to remind you.
* **💥 Explosive Click:** Clicking it triggers a satisfying visual explosion.

---

### 🔥 Espo Fury — Real Flames

* **🌋 No More Blobs:** The Fury flames no longer look like plain yellow circles. They now have a teardrop shape with a natural gradient from white to red.
* **💨 Motion Effect:** The flames move organically, with flicker and palpable heat, fed by a rippling haze on the ground.

---

### 🍄 Super Espò — Authentic Retro Look

* **🌍 Animated Scenery:** The central panel now hosts a complete Super Mario scene — sky, clouds, mountains, pipes, bushes and a brick floor, all scrolling infinitely without jumps.
* **🎨 Pixel-Art Aesthetic:** Thick black borders, stepped shadows, classic colors (red/yellow/green/brown) and the Press Start 2P font. Fully retro style.
* **🎮 Themed Components:** Brick-brown navbar, yellow tabs with red active state, pipe-green "buy" buttons, underground-blue upgrade cards. Every game element breathes Super Mario.
* **🪙 Reworked `?` Block:** Mario's classic mystery block is back for the golden bug, with perfect floating.
* **🎯 Visible Quantity Selector:** The purchase multiplier (1x/5x/10x/MAX) now clearly shows which one is selected with a Mario-red style.

---

### 🎬 Cinematic Boot Intro

* **💻 "Debug → Deploy" Sequence:** Logging in now triggers a short hacker-style intro — Matrix-style code rain, a command typed on screen in real time, a glitch and finally the logo reveal.
* **🎧 Custom Audio:** Dedicated sound effects (glitch, scanner, "fixed", reveal) and background music that kicks in at just the right moment, at the end of the intro.
* **⚡ Only When It Matters:** The intro plays only on an actual login — no sequence on refresh or automatic re-entry, so it never slows you down.

### 🌍 Play in Italian or English

* **🗣️ Language Selector:** You can now choose the game's language (Italian / English) right from your profile.
* **🌐 Translated Everywhere:** Interface, options, arcade and even these release notes follow your chosen language.

### 🏆 18 New Achievements (meme & nerd themed)

### 🎄 Refined Christmas Theme

## Version 2.0: Format, New Horizons and Arcade 2.0!

This isn't a simple update, it's a true generational leap. Version 2.0 (which also includes the unreleased content from patches 1.2 and 1.3) introduces "New Game Plus", pushes the endgame toward unimaginable numbers, expands the Arcade and completely overhauls the game's aesthetic, structural and performance management.

---

### 🌌 The Great Leap (Endgame & Gameplay)

* **🔄 THE FORMAT (New Game Plus):** The real revolution of this update! Once you reach a certain limit, you can "Format" the system. This resets your progress but rewards you with a **new exclusive currency**, letting you unlock **new upgrades** and **rare skins**. The real game starts now!
* **🚀 Math Expansion (Up to Qag!):** The mathematical infrastructure has been upgraded. Added new suffixes for astronomical numbers up to *Quadragintillion* (1e125). We also implemented strict checks on scientific values to avoid endgame breakage.
* **📱 Install the Official App (PWA):** Espòòò Clicker becomes a full-fledged application! You can now install it directly on your smartphone or PC. It supports offline play, instant loading and invisible background updates.
* **⚖️ Promotion & LAB Balancing:** The difficulty curve has been softened. The cost increase for Promotions goes from 5x to **2.5x**. Added exponential calculation for LAB and Promotion, with the current value shown in the statistics. LAB costs rounded for better readability.

---

### 🕹️ Arcade Mania 2.0

* **👾 New Minigames:** **Asteroids** — a great classic joins the Arcade family. **Super Espò Run** — get ready to run and jump in this new adventure.
* **🎮 Rebalanced Super Espò Runner:** Completely reworked gameplay with more precise physics, improved level generation and new scenery elements. Now every run is a fair challenge!
* **🎨 Total Arcade Restyle:** New dedicated UI, new exclusive sounds for the arcade and improved safe closing of the minigames.
* **🚀 Space Impacts:** Performed a code cleanup and a general difficulty rebalance of the minigame.

---

### 🎭 Aesthetics & Customization

* **🆕 New Exclusive Skins:** Formatting unlocks access to **new skins** of Epic and Legendary rarity, some with hidden surprises... 👀
* **🎤 New Mystery Event:** A new video event joins the family. Can you recognize it?
* **🖼️ Stylish & Mobile-Ready Leaderboard:** The global leaderboard now shows your **profile picture** based on your last equipped skin, plus your badges. The display has been rewritten to lay out perfectly on smartphone screens, with no cut-off text!
* **👗 Renewed Wardrobe:** The skin menu has been redesigned with a darker, more modern look, smoother navigation and improved sorting. Reclaimed precious space by optimizing the gallery to show more information.
* **🌈 Dynamic Theme Management:** The graphics engine now dynamically loads and manages the aesthetic themes tied to skins, reshaping the game's look smoothly (Phase 2 Improvement).
* **🏆 UI & Details:** Achievements tied to past events will be hidden "off-season", keeping the board tidy. Also added a new dedicated UI for the Promotion menu.

---

### ✨ Visual & Interaction Restyle (UI 2.0)

* **🖥️ Full-Screen Layout:** The game now fills the whole screen, with no container frames. More space, a more immersive look.
* **📐 Unified Navbar:** All navigation bar buttons have the same width for a tidier, more professional look.
* **📲 Install App Button:** Appears automatically in the navbar when the browser supports PWA installation.
* **🔘 Renewed Clicker Button:** The main button shines with an animated ping ring and a breathing glow halo.
* **🔥 Visual Combo System:** The faster you click, the more the game reacts! Once you reach the x5 multiplier, a dynamic combo counter appears, changing color and size based on click intensity.
* **💫 Explosive Click Feedback:** The new sparkling particles are no longer static — they grow in number and scale as your combo rises!
* **🌟 Epic Promotion:** Performing a Promotion now triggers a spectacular sequence with flashes, screen shake and orchestrated animations.
* **📱 Optimized Spaces (Mobile):** Thinner navigation bars, reduced padding and an **enlarged main clicker button** for a flawless touch experience.
* **🎬 Premium Details:** New cascading entrance animations, buttons with depth and light effects, dynamic score and an overall more "cyber" look.

---

### 🎨 Refined Wardrobe (UI Polish)

* **💎 Uniform Cards:** Grid mode — all cards have equal height, regardless of content (locked or unlocked). Clean, tidy layout.
* **✨ Flash on Skin Change:** Carousel mode — a glowing ring that expands when you select a skin, colored by rarity. Elegant and intuitive visual.
* **⌨️ Key Navigation:** UP/DOWN arrows jump between rarities without filters, allowing fast exploration of the collection. Continuous loop for smooth navigation.

---

### 🔥 Empowered Espo Fury

* **🌋 Renewed Flame Effect:** The Espo Fury animation has been completely revised with a multi-layer crown of fire. An expanding fire ring, an animated warm orange halo and more intense edge flashes — the clicker has never looked so dangerous.

---

### 🎭 Theme Fixes

* **◼️ 8-bit Theme — Navbar Overlap Fixed:** Corrected the 5px overlap between the navbar (70px) and the game content.
* **◼️ Consistent Square Clicker:** In the 8-bit and Super themes, the clicker button is now fully square: border, halo, ring and explosive particles included.
* **🟢 Matrix — Isolated Text:** The green terminal style now applies only inside the game, without coloring the navbar or modals.
* **🔵 Bluescreen — Hidden Grid:** The background grid is now correctly hidden during the blue screen event.

---

### 🔊 Audio Engine 2.0 & Fixes

* **🎵 Next-Gen Audio Engine:** Implemented a powerful new structured audio engine. No more stutters: enjoy smooth crossfade transitions between tracks and instant response from sound effects during your most frantic clicks.
* **🎵 New Track:** Added "Divine Music" to the Jukebox.
* **🔇 Volume & Controls Fix:** Fixed the bug that blasted audio at max after reloading the page. The master volume slider and the "Mute" button now respond instantly. Muting pauses instead of restarting the track. Fixed music IDs tied to some specific skins and fixed the *fireball* audio during the Fury ability.

---

### ⚙️ Engine, Security & Under the Hood

* **🛡️ Anti-Rollback System v3:** Your saves are locked down. We introduced a strict control hierarchy (Format > Promotion > Score) that absolutely prevents overwriting with old data.
* **💾 Production Leaderboard Fix:** Fixed a critical bug that prevented the leaderboard from updating in production due to a missing database column. Scores now sync correctly.
* **🚀 Performance & Goodbye Lag:** Removed heavy external modules and packages in favor of lightweight in-house code. Lighter game calculations, optimized canvases and graphics now GPU-accelerated.
* **🔄 Perfect Synchronization:** Added a forced save right before opening the Global Leaderboard: your online score will always be up to date to the last click. Fixed critical bugs with mobile saves and background restores (Service Worker).
* **🧹 Total Cleanup on Reset:** Using the "Delete Data" option now permanently destroys backup saves too, restores the base skin and lets you start a pristine adventure.
* **🔒 Security & Tokens:** Added session tokens to protect accounts and prevent abnormal disconnections.
* **⚡ Loading & File System:** The old *Game-Data* monolith has been organized into multiple modules, the CSS split per platform to avoid forcing the layout, and all heavy menus now enjoy dynamic loading for lightning-fast game starts.
